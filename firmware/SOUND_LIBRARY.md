# NIGHTFALL original sound bank

These are **original parameter patches**, generated by NIGHTFALL's own
oscillator, envelope and FX code. No movie soundtrack recording, Yamaha preset
data, or third-party sample is included. Each song preset loads four voices:

| Song | Drum | Bass | Chord | Lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MIDNIGHT · 96 BPM | TUNNEL NOISE | SHADOW PULSE | NOIR BRASS | GLASS SIGNAL |
| NEON RUN · 112 BPM | MOTOR GRID | VOLT SAW | CHROME PAD | LASER EDGE |
| AFTER RAIN · 84 BPM | DISTANT STATIC | WET SUB | FOG CHOIR | RAIN BELL |

The patches deliberately contrast: MIDNIGHT uses triangle lead and slow brass
envelopes; NEON RUN uses brighter saw/square attacks and drive; AFTER RAIN uses
subdued sine, wide pad and an original ring-wave bell. The first four
waveform indices now match the browser's sine/triangle/saw/square order. Drum
keys produce distinct original kick, snare and hat synthesis rather than one
undifferentiated noise timbre. Macro defaults and
FX enable/mix change with the song. The waveforms are mathematical synth
shapes; the ring wave is calculated from two triangle phases, not a sample.

The broad analogue-brass and metallic-ring palette takes inspiration from the
CS-80 sound-design context documented by
[Yamaha](https://ca.yamaha.com/en/business/audio/resources/self-training/micro-tutorial/20170525/)
and its [ring-modulation history](https://se.yamaha.com/sv/musical-instruments/keyboards/explore/montage-portal/essentials/cs.html).
These sources are aesthetic references only, not licensed sound assets.

For a **later optional sample/wavetable extension**, two clear-license sources
are [Adventure Kid Waveforms (AKWF-FREE)](https://github.com/KristofferKarlAxelEkstrand/AKWF-FREE)
for compact CC0 single-cycle oscillators and
[Versilian VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/)
for CC0 brass, strings and bells. Neither is bundled here: the firmware does
not yet have a qualified sample streaming/storage path, and bulk downloading
assets would not make a safe FM-1 build. Any future selection should record
exact source file, revision, conversion settings and license in the package.

Run `make audio-test` to render and check three 12-second stereo WAV excerpts.
These previews prove that the host synthesis path is audible and distinct, not
that this revised candidate has passed a physical listening test.
